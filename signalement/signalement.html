JUDICIAL PROCEDURE

WHO SHOULD YOU REPORT TO?

Anyone may report an incident or file a complaint with the public prosecutor.

File a complaint at a police station.

Join proceedings as a civil party (victim, rights holders, sports federation).

In an emergency, call 17. You can also file a complaint or use the online pre-reporting and chat service at arretonslesviolences.fr.

WHO CAN BE SUBJECT TO A REPORT?

Any person implicated (members of the general public included).

ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURE

WHO SHOULD YOU REPORT TO?

Report to Signal Sports, the ministerial unit that receives and handles reports of violence in sport.

SIGNAL-SPORTS@sports.gouv.fr

Report to the local SDJES departmental youth, engagement and sports services.

WHO CAN BE SUBJECT TO A REPORT?

Professional sports educators, paid or volunteer.

Referees and judges.

DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE

WHO SHOULD YOU REPORT TO?

Report to the relevant sports federation or federations.

WHO CAN BE SUBJECT TO A REPORT?

Any person implicated who holds a licence issued by the relevant federation or federations.
